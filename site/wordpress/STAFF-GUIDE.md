# APTERS Website: Staff Guide

Your website runs on WordPress. You do not need any coding skills. Changes you make appear on the site straight away.

## Logging in
1. Go to **your-domain.org/wp-admin**.
2. Enter the username and password you were given.

## Adding an event
1. Click **Events** > **Add New**.
2. Type the event name in the title box and the description below it.
3. Click **Set featured image** on the right and upload a photo.
4. In the **Event details** box on the right, pick the **Date** and type the **Place**.
5. Click **Publish**.

Events appear on the home page and on the Events page. Once the date has passed, they drop off the home page automatically.

## Updating the team
1. Click **Team** > **Add New** (or open an existing person).
2. Enter the name, and their job title in the **Role** box.
3. Add a photo as the featured image, and a short description in the main box.
4. Click **Publish**. Use **Order** (Page Attributes) to change the order.

## Sponsorship prices
1. Click **Sponsorships** > **Add New** (or edit an existing one).
2. Enter the item name (for example "Special chair"), a short description, and the price in the **Price (KW)** box (numbers only, e.g. 850).
3. Click **Publish**.

## Changing contact details, bank details, and the home photo
Click **Appearance** > **Customize** > **APTERS Settings**. You can change:
- the home headline and intro text
- the home photo (upload a wide photo; landscape works best)
- email, phone, office hours, address
- bank details for donations
- Facebook and Instagram links

Press **Publish** at the top when you are done.

## Pages (About, Contact, etc.)
Click **Pages** to edit existing pages, or **Add New** to create one. Use the **+** button to add headings, photos and text.

## Good to know
- Photos: resize large phone photos before uploading if possible (under about 2 MB).
- If something looks wrong, take a screenshot and send it to the web team.
