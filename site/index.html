Founded in Lusaka in 1990

APTERS (Appropriate Paper Technology) is a non-profit. We make special chairs, standing frames, walking frames, wedges and rollers for children with neurological or physical disabilities, such as cerebral palsy, spina bifida and birth injuries.

We also give paid work to adults with disabilities and to mothers of disabled children.

“My passion is helping people. I like people with disabilities especially children. I am so passionate because I went through when I was younger.”

Kenneth Habaalu, Executive Director and founder

Each device is measured, cut and sewn by hand

Every child is measured at our clinic on Wednesdays. The device is built to that measurement, with room to grow.

  1. Measure

    Parents bring the child to our Wednesday clinic. Measurements are taken for each piece of equipment, with removable footrests to allow for growth.

  2. Cut

    Measurements are transferred to strong cardboard. A template is cut and glued into the basic shape, then dried, usually in the sun.

  3. Paste

    Three layers of recycled white paper and a final layer of brown paper give strength and a smooth surface for painting.

  4. Paint and strap

    Devices are painted in APTERS blue. Straps are sewn so the child sits or stands in the best position, and for safety.

  5. Hand over

    Parents are shown how to position the child and fasten the straps, then practise under supervision.
