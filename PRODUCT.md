## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, deployed to GitHub Pages. The user asked for a plain HTML page rather than WordPress for this redesign.

## Users

Three equally important audiences, each with a clear route from the top of the page:
- Parents and carers of children with neurological or physical disabilities, looking for a mobility device for their child.
- Donors and sponsors who pay for a device, or give materials or money.
- Volunteers, schools, companies and partners who want to help, host a talk, or collaborate.

## Product Purpose

APTERS (Appropriate Paper Technology) is a Lusaka-based non-profit, founded in 1990, that makes low-cost custom mobility and assistive devices (special chairs, standing frames, walking frames, therapy wedges and rollers) from recycled paper and cardboard. It also gives paid work to adults with disabilities and mothers of disabled children. Success means families get devices they cannot otherwise afford, and more people sponsor, donate, volunteer and partner.

## Positioning

Devices made from recycled paper that are measured for each child, fitted at a weekly clinic, and handed over with parents trained to use them. Most families cannot pay for a device; sponsors can pay for one for a named child, who receives a certificate with the child's name.

## Operating Context

- A weekly measuring clinic at University Teaching Hospital (UTH), Lusaka, on Wednesdays.
- Production in the workshop: cutting, pasting three layers of white paper plus a brown layer, painting in APTERS standard blue, sewing straps.
- Craft shop in Pa Mpofu craft market, Bauleni, Tuesday to Sunday 9:00 to 17:00; craft markets including ZADS.
- Contact: office hours Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site content is edited by staff in WordPress (a theme exists in the project). This redesign is a static HTML page; content changes are made in the HTML.
- Sponsorship prices in KW: special chair 850, standing frame 1,200, walking frame 1,750, therapy wedge 1,500, therapy roller 1,500.
- Bank details for donations are provided in the source document and must be shown exactly.
- Donation form: opens the visitor's email app; no server.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: APTERS, Appropriate Paper Technology.
- Logo: the APTERS logo with a wheelchair user in a recycling loop, in blue (#27348b-family) and green.
- Standard device colour: APTERS blue.
- Voice: plain, warm, respectful of disabled people. Describe people with dignity and ability, not pity. Use "children with disabilities", not "suffering" or "special needs".

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photos in project assets: children and devices at clinics and events, team portraits (12), workshop, shop, awards certificate, building.
- Team bios and roles from the source document.
- Partners and friends list with logos for 16 of 39 partners; the rest are name-only and must not get invented logos.
- Quotes from Kenneth Habaalu and Karen Edwards-Jellis exist in the source document and may be used verbatim.
- Absences that must not be fabricated: customer counts beyond "4,000 items annually", testimonials from families, measured outcomes, any claim beyond the source document.

## Product Principles

- Show the real child, the real workshop and the real paper. Never stock photos.
- Each audience gets a route in the first screen: request a device, sponsor one, or help.
- Price and bank facts are shown plainly, never hidden behind menus.
- The page stays accessible on a phone and works without JavaScript for its core content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Users include disabled people and families with disabled children: keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced-motion respect, and plain language.
